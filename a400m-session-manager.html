Simulator Session Manager

A practical tool built alongside my day-to-day test & integration work on the A400M full-flight simulator at Airbus Defence & Space — designed to make the preparation before every test session faster, cleaner and less error-prone.

What it does

Guides and structures the setup work that has to happen before each simulator test session — the configurations, checks and inputs the sim needs so that when the test slot opens, everything is already in place and the team can go straight into testing instead of troubleshooting.

Why it matters

Simulator time is scarce and expensive, and a single misconfigured parameter can burn hours of a shared session. The tool turns what used to be scattered notes and tacit knowledge into a repeatable, checkable process, cutting prep time and reducing the risk of a lost session.

Context

Built in a security-cleared defense environment, kept deliberately simple and maintainable so the team could rely on it day to day. A small example of removing friction from the real engineering work happening around me.
