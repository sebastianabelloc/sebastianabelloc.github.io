Simulator Session Manager

A practical tool built alongside my day-to-day test & integration work on the A400M full-flight simulator at Airbus Defence & Space.

What it does

Plans and manages simulator test-session scheduling across the team — centralising bookings, reducing double-booking and coordination overhead, and giving clear visibility of simulator availability.

Context

Built in a security-cleared defense environment, kept deliberately simple and maintainable so the team could rely on it day to day. A small example of removing friction from the real engineering work happening around me.
