About

Final-year Aerospace Engineering student at Universidad Europea de Madrid, currently working as a Flight Simulator Test & Integration Engineer on the A400M program at Airbus Defence & Space (through CT Engineering).

I work where flight dynamics, control systems and code meet. Designing and modelling the guidance and control of autonomous vehicles is the work I care about most — and the direction I'm building toward.

Bilingual (Spanish / English), EU work-eligible, and always glad to talk with people working across space and defense.
